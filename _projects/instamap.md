<p align="justify">
    <code>InstaMap</code> is an AI-powered tool that turns your saved Instagram posts into an interactive map. It 
    automatically downloads your posts, geotags them, and uses Google’s Gemini AI to uncover hidden 
    locations in the captions. The result? A beautiful, explorable map that lets you revisit memories, 
    discover patterns, and see your Instagram world like never before — all fully automated with 
    GitHub Actions.
</p>
