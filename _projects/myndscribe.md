<p align="justify">
    <code>Myndscribe</code> is an AI-powered personal knowledge curator that reads emails you send to yourself, 
    extracts their content, analyzes attached links, and creates a concise summary. All your self-sent 
    thoughts are transformed into a beautifully organized web interface — making it easy to rediscover, 
    reflect, and build your second brain.
</p>
